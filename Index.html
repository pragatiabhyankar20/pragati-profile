About

I'm a graphic designer in the Pune district, working remotely as a professional graphic designer at Yuktee. I make social posts, videos and marketing creatives for clients, and I enjoy turning a brief into clear, memorable visuals.

After a BBA and a short stint as a career counsellor, I trained in graphic design at Arena Animation Indore in 2022. Since then I've designed for agencies and clients across social media, e-commerce, print and packaging.
